# Playable Nightmares

**An emerging video game genre originally created and proposed by NotAnsh.**

> You don't watch the nightmare. You experience it.

## What Is Playable Nightmares?

Playable Nightmares is a proposed video game genre focused on creating interactive experiences that make players feel as though they are personally experiencing a nightmare.

These experiences can originate from real dreams or be entirely imagined. They can explore psychological horror, ghosts, surreal environments, liminal spaces, creatures, and other forms of horror.

The defining feature is not the setting, visual style, or type of monster. It is the experience itself.

## Core Principles

- **Experiential:** The player should feel like they are experiencing a nightmare, rather than simply watching one.
- **Interactive:** The player actively participates through gameplay.
- **Atmospheric:** Environments, sounds, events, and gameplay contribute to the nightmare experience.
- **Unrestricted:** Nightmares can be realistic, surreal, psychological, supernatural, or a combination of different horror styles.

## Qualification Criteria

A game should satisfy all three requirements to be considered a Playable Nightmare:

1. **Nightmare Centrality:** The nightmare experience must be central to the game's identity.
2. **Nightmare Experience Design:** The game must deliberately create a sustained nightmare-like experience through its design.
3. **Interactive Participation:** The player must actively experience and participate in the nightmare through gameplay.

## What Can a Playable Nightmare Be?

Playable Nightmares is an umbrella genre. It can include:

- Psychological horror
- Ghost and supernatural horror
- Surreal horror
- Liminal horror
- Creature horror
- Cosmic horror
- Survival horror
- Exploration-based horror
and more!
These are possible approaches, not mandatory subgenres or restrictions.

## What Does Not Automatically Qualify?

Not every horror game is a Playable Nightmare.

A conventional horror game does not qualify simply because it contains a dream sequence, strange visuals, or a frightening monster.

The nightmare experience must be central to the overall game.
