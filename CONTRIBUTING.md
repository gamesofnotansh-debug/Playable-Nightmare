# Contributing to Playable Nightmares

Thank you for your interest in the Playable Nightmares genre.

This project is maintained by its original creator, **NotAnsh**.

## Suggesting Improvements

Anyone can share feedback, ideas, questions, or suggestions regarding the genre.

You can do this by:

- Opening a GitHub Issue.
- Starting a discussion.
- Suggesting clarification to existing criteria.
- Sharing examples of games that may qualify.

## Changes to Official Specifications

The official definition, qualification criteria, and other core documents are maintained by the project owner.

Suggestions do not automatically become part of the official specification.

**All changes to the official project require approval from the project owner.**

## Community Participation

Developers are free to create Playable Nightmare games, experiment with the genre, and share their work.

Participation does not grant ownership of this project or authority to change its official specifications.

## Thank You

The goal is to help Playable Nightmares grow into a recognizable genre through creativity, experimentation, and community participation.
