# Playable Nightmares — Qualification Criteria

**Version:** 1.0  
**Originally proposed by:** NotAnsh

## Purpose

These criteria define what makes a video game a Playable Nightmare. They help distinguish the genre from horror games that may share similar themes, visuals, or mechanics.

A game does not need to follow a particular art style, perspective, setting, or horror subgenre.

## The Three Core Requirements

### 1. Nightmare Centrality

The nightmare experience must be central to the identity of the game.

A short dream sequence, optional nightmare level, or isolated hallucination is not enough if the rest of the game is fundamentally unrelated.

### 2. Nightmare Experience Design

The game must deliberately create an experience associated with nightmares.

This may involve:

- Disorientation and confusion.
- A sense of vulnerability or helplessness.
- Disturbing familiarity.
- Impossible or changing environments.
- Unexplained and unsettling events.
- Dread, fear, or psychological discomfort.
- Situations that defy ordinary expectations.

These are examples, not a checklist. A game does not need to include all of them.

### 3. Interactive Participation

The player must actively experience the nightmare through gameplay.

The experience should involve player interaction, decisions, movement, exploration, survival, puzzles, or other gameplay activities rather than relying entirely on passive cutscenes.

## Flexibility

Playable Nightmares does not require:

- A real dream as its source.
- Dream logic or surreal environments.
- Ghosts, monsters, or supernatural entities.
- A particular visual style.
- First-person gameplay.
- A specific narrative structure.
- Jumpscares or combat.

A completely realistic environment can still qualify if the overall experience creates the feeling of a nightmare.

## Qualification Test

Ask three questions:

1. Is the nightmare experience central to the game's identity?
2. Does the game deliberately create a sustained nightmare-like experience?
3. Does the player actively participate in that experience through gameplay?

A game should satisfy all three requirements to qualify.

## Relationship With Other Genres

Playable Nightmares is an umbrella genre. A game may also belong to psychological horror, survival horror, adventure, exploration, puzzle, or other genres.

## Final Principle

**The defining feature of a Playable Nightmare is the experience it creates, not the individual elements it contains.**
